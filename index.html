Yuval's Notes

Lecture notes from some courses I've taken over the years. Feel free to reach out if you find a typo (big or small).

Fall 2026 (In Progress)

  • MATH730: Algebraic Topology I CW complexes; fundamental group; covering spaces; simplicial, singular, and cellular homology.

    Instructor: Or Hershkovitz

    Book: Algebraic Topology, Hatcher

  • MATH606: Complex Algebraic Geometry Riemann surfaces; elliptic functions; the Riemann surface of an algebraic function; Čech cohomology; vector bundles; divisors; Riemann-Roch; Serre duality.

    Instructor: Harry Tamvakis

    Book: Compact Riemann Surfaces, Narasimhan

  • STAT410: Intro to Probability Theory Basic facts about σ-algebras and probability measures; conditional probability; random variables; expectation; central limit theorem; law of large numbers.

    Instructor: Mestiyage D. Gunatilleka

    Book: Intro to Probability, Anderson

Spring 2026

  • MATH601: Abstract Algebra II Exterior powers; FGMs over PIDs; bilinear forms; categories; Yoneda's lemma; projective, injective, and flat modules; homological algebra; Ext and Tor; Galois theory; solvability by radicals; representations; Schur's lemma; character tables and orthogonality relations; Burnside's theorem; additional topics in rep. theory.

    Instructor: Harry Tamvakis

    Book: Abstract Algebra, Dummit and Foote
